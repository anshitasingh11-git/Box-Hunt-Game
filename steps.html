How to Play

Welcome to Box Hunt!

🎯 Find the Box

Find the pink box inside the game area and click it as quickly as you can.

⏱ Beat the Timer

You have 30 seconds to play the game.

⭐ Earn Points

Every successful click increases your score by 1 point.

🔄 Box Moves

After every successful click, the box moves to a new random position.

🏆 Goal

Score as many points as possible before the 30-second timer runs out!
